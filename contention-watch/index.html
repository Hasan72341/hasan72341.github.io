Private research utility

Inter IIT Contention Watch

Inter IIT Contention Watch monitors public GitHub repositories for material relevant to Inter IIT technical problem statements. It keeps an evidence-based inventory of candidate projects, selection resources, changes, and discovery coverage. Automated classifications are provisional and can be reviewed by the owner.

Why the app connects to Google

The owner authorizes the app to create and update a private Google Sheets tracker. The app writes its inventory and health information, reads manual decisions from the tracker’s Review tab, and shares the tracker with a collaborator explicitly selected by the owner.

The app requests the drive.file permission. This permission covers only specific Google Drive files created by or explicitly used with this app. It does not request general access to the owner’s Drive, Gmail, or Calendar.

Who can use it

This is a privately operated utility for its owner and known collaborators. There is no public account registration. The repository inventory and the Google Sheets tracker are not published on this informational website.
