Inter IIT Contention Watch

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026. Inter IIT Contention Watch is a private repository research utility operated by its owner, reachable at 6hasanraza9@gmail.com.

Google data the app uses

After the owner grants consent, the app uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and maintain its tracker spreadsheet. It checks the authorizing account’s email address to verify the intended owner, reads spreadsheet structure and app-managed values, reads the tracker’s Review tab, and reads sharing permissions to confirm access is limited to the owner and authorized collaborators.

The app writes repository findings, source links, classification evidence, change records, and operational health information to its own spreadsheet. It reads manual review decisions so the owner’s corrections can be reflected in later reports. It does not request access to unrelated Google Drive files, email messages, or calendars.

Purpose and storage

Google data is used only to operate and maintain this private tracker, verify its owner, apply review decisions, and diagnose synchronization failures. The owner’s server stores the spreadsheet identifier, synchronization state, review decisions, and necessary account metadata. OAuth refresh credentials are stored separately in private files with restricted operating-system permissions; they are not included in repository source code or public reports.

The repository inventory also contains information from public GitHub repositories and public owner profiles. These public-source findings are separate from the Google authorization data.

Sharing and use limits

The tracker is shared only with collaborators explicitly selected by the owner. The app does not make the spreadsheet publicly accessible. Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. It is not transferred to unrelated parties. Authorized operators may inspect the tracker and synchronization state when reviewing findings or diagnosing a problem.

Inter IIT Contention Watch’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Synchronization state and review information are retained while the owner operates the tracker. The owner can stop the watcher, revoke its access through Google Account connections, delete its local credentials and stored state, and delete the tracker from Google Drive. Revoking access stops future API access; it does not automatically delete existing spreadsheets or stored review information.

To request removal of stored information or discuss access, contact 6hasanraza9@gmail.com. Google also handles data under its own privacy policy and account settings.
